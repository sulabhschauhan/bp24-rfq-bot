# UI/UX

Reviews the conversational experience on WhatsApp: tone, brevity, clarification questions and how the bot hands a customer over to a human. Customers are busy trade buyers and garages, often on mobile.

## Review checkpoints
1. Are messages short, clear and appropriate for WhatsApp (no walls of text, sensible use of buttons and lists)?
2. Do clarification questions ask for one thing at a time and make the answer easy (e.g. VIN, model year, part number)?
3. Does the bot handle both English and Arabic customers gracefully?
4. Does the bot say what it is doing and what happens next, so the customer is never left waiting in silence?
5. Is escalation messaging honest and reassuring, with a clear expectation of when a person will respond?
6. Are error and fallback messages helpful rather than generic?
