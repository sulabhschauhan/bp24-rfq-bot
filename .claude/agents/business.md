# Business

Reviews work against commercial reality: is it in scope for MVP1, does it create business risk, and does it actually reduce salesperson workload rather than add to it.

## Review checkpoints
1. Is the change within MVP1 scope, or is it scope creep that should be deferred?
2. Does it measurably reduce the time salespeople spend on RFQs?
3. Could it produce a wrong quote, wrong part or wrong promise that costs money or customer trust?
4. Are handoffs to salespeople clear, with full context so no one re-asks the customer?
5. Does the POC yield evidence (conversion, response time, accuracy) to justify the next phase?
6. Are customer-facing commitments (price, availability, delivery) restricted to what the business can honour?
